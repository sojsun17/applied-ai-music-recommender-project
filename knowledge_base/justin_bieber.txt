Justin Bieber is a Canadian pop star who rose to fame at age 15 after being discovered on YouTube in 2008. Known for his smooth R&B-influenced pop sound, Bieber's music ranges from teenage anthems to mature introspective ballads. His early hits like "Baby" are defined by high danceability and upbeat energy, while later albums explored darker emotional territory. He is one of the best-selling music artists of all time with over 150 million records sold worldwide.
