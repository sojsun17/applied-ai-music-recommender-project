Johnny Cash was an American country music legend born in Kingsland, Arkansas in 1932. Known as "The Man in Black," Cash's music spanned country, rock and roll, rockabilly, blues, and gospel. His cover of Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt," recorded in 2002 near the end of his life, is considered one of the most emotionally devastating recordings in popular music history. The sparse acoustic production and Cash's weathered voice transforms the original into a quiet meditation on regret, legacy, and mortality.
