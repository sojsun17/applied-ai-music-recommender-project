Radiohead is an English rock band formed in Abingdon, Oxfordshire in 1985. The band is known for their melancholic, introspective sound that blends alternative rock, art rock, and electronic music. Their 1992 single "Creep" became a defining anthem of alienation and self-doubt, with its quiet-loud dynamic and Thom Yorke's haunting falsetto. Radiohead's music is characterised by slow-building tension, sparse guitar textures, and emotionally raw lyrical themes exploring loneliness, anxiety, and disconnection.
